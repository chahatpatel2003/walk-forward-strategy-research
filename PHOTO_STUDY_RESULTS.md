**Setup.**
- **Account model: constrained.** If the account's marked equity reaches $0, pending orders are cancelled, any position is closed at the next open, and no new trades are taken (also in later walk-forward periods). In the normal runs only A12 on gold futures hit this; in the stress tests A12 also hit it on NAS100 and spot gold (see below). A run that hits $0 anywhere cannot pass the key checks.
- **Account and sizing:** $100,000 account. Daily strategies buy $100,000 of contract value per trade (whole futures contracts, 0.01-lot CFDs), with no leverage and no compounding.
- **Costs:**
  - futures: commission + 1 tick slippage per side, plus the roll cost whenever a position is held through a contract switch
  - XAUUSD: the real bid/ask spread
  - NAS100: a 1.2-point spread
- **Fills:** the next bar's open after the signal.
- **Walk-forward:** daily strategies optimise on 36 months and trade the next 12; intraday 18 → 6.
- **Monte Carlo:** 5,000 bootstrap runs.
- **Buy-and-hold benchmark:** $100,000 of contract value (whole contracts) over exactly the same scored periods, with the same costs, roll charges and drawdown method as the strategies.
  - For the walk-forward comparison it holds **one continuous position** through back-to-back out-of-sample windows (64 runs).
  - It closes only at a real gap, where a fold had no valid parameter set (3 runs: re-bought after the gap).
- **Verification:** results were recomputed independently from the raw data files, without the engine.
  - Turn-of-month on NAS100 (cash session): all 114 closed trades are identical to the cent; the engine's one extra trade is closed at the end of the data ($83,545.99 − $3,475.20 = $80,070.79, exactly the study row).
  - A1 on MNQ (cash session): the same 41 trades and the same net P&L ($23,224.00).
  - The reviewer's independent replay of A10 on MGC with one decision per session (+$104,129.87) matched the previous round exactly. With the new causal gold roll estimates the row is now +$103,849.57.
  - The reviewer's continuous buy-and-hold for A1 on NAS100's out-of-sample period (+$253,118.36, −$71,002.14 drawdown) matches this study's benchmark exactly.
  - Every scored walk-forward fold's day count equals its scored days: 355 folds in the 68 runs that had out-of-sample results (67 single-market runs plus the B01 pair), 0 mismatches. (In the other 26 runs no fold had a parameter set with enough trades, so there was nothing to score.)

## Verdict by strategy

"Passed all key checks" means all of these:
- profitable out-of-sample with profit factor ≥ 1.2
- at most one of the 13 checklist items not passed
- under 10% Monte Carlo chance of finishing at a loss
- still profitable under the combined execution stress **and** in every single stress scenario (costs ×2, slippage +1/+2 ticks, spread ×1.5/×2/×3, 1- or 2-bar delay)
- the account never hit $0 (full history, walk-forward, the combined stress or any single stress scenario)
- the walk-forward efficiency item counts as not passed when the ratio is not credible (fewer than half the folds scored, or in-sample profit under 1% of capital a year)

## Important limitations
1. **CFD overnight financing ("swap") is not modelled.** XAUUSD and NAS100 positions held overnight would really pay financing, often several % a year on long positions. Results on those two markets are overstated for strategies that hold for weeks (A8, A10, A11). Futures (MNQ, MGC) don't have this problem, because financing is built into the futures price and roll costs are charged.
2. **Bull-market sample.** Gold and the Nasdaq mostly rose in 2012–2026. Long-only dip-buying is exactly what a rising market rewards. The 2022 bear market is inside the MNQ data and the dip-buyers survived it, but a long sideways or falling market is under-represented.
3. **Many tests, some luck.** 94 runs and parameter grids give plenty of chances for something to look good by accident. Trust the strategies that worked **across markets and bar types**, not the single best row.
4. **Some rules are my assumptions** (marked ⚠ in each strategy file and in `STRATEGY_CATALOG.md`): A2, A3, A4, A6, A7 exits, A8 exit, A11 exit, A12 entirely. If the creator meant something else, the results may differ.
5. **"Buy at the close" rules** (A6, A7, A9 in the photos) are executed at the **next open**; we can't trade a close we've just observed.
6. **Decision days:** daily strategies make decisions only on exchange trading days. On futures/CFD holiday sessions (full-session bars) they hold but don't trade.
7. **Photo claims vs ours, A1 as an example:** the photo claims a 78.6% win rate and PF 3.94 (QQQ 1999–2026, no costs). We got 80.5% wins and PF 2.50 on MNQ (2021–2026, cash session), and 81–82% wins with PF 2.75–3.53 on NAS100 (2012–2021). The win rates match; the profit factors are lower after costs.

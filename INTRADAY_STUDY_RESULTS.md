# Intraday study: the photo strategies, flat every day by 15:55 ET

*Generated November 23, 2026 by `tools/intraday_report.py` from `results/INTRADAY_STUDY/rows.csv` (144 runs, 0 errors).*

## How it was run

* **Same strategies, same entry and exit rules** as the daily and timeframe studies, on 1-, 5-, 15-minute and 1-hour bars of the **New York cash session (09:30–16:00)**. Lengths stay in bars; thresholds written as a % of price are scaled by √(bar length ÷ original bar length), as in the timeframe study.
* **Intraday:** every open position is closed so the exit fills at the last bar starting by 15:55 (12:55 on half days): 15:55 on 1- and 5-minute bars, 15:45 on 15-minute, 15:00 on 1-hour. No new entry from that point; orders expire at the end of the day. Nothing is held overnight (checked on every trade below).
* Strategies: A01–A07, A11, A12. A08–A10 decide once a week or month (can't be intraday); A13 is intraday already (timeframe study); the B01 pair is not included.
* **Same pipeline:** full history, parameter grid with the look-ahead gate, walk-forward (18 months in-sample → 6 out-of-sample), Monte Carlo, 9 stress scenarios, 13-item checklist; $100,000 of contract value per trade, costs as before. **"Passed every check"** is the same rule as the other studies.
